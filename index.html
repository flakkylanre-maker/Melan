Meet SIMI

Ask anything. Get a straight answer.

A product, a routine step, a mark that won't fade — SIMI answers plainly. No lectures, no upsell, and never a word about lightening your skin.

what can shea butter do to my skin

Shea seals. It doesn't do much active work on marks itself, but it locks water into the skin so nothing you put underneath evaporates off — that's what stops ashiness and keeps the barrier intact enough to heal marks instead of making new ones.

What it won't do is fade a mark on its own or protect you from sun. It's the last step, not the treatment.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is MELAN free?

Yes. MELAN is free to download, and you can scan, check products and ask SIMI for free every week. MELAN Pro removes the limits and unlocks your full routine and progress photos — and new accounts get 5 days of Pro to try it.

Does MELAN keep my photos?

No. Your scan photo is read once and discarded — only the scores are saved. If you keep progress photos, they stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

Is MELAN only for dark skin?

MELAN is built starting with melanin-rich skin — the skin most apps read wrong — and reads every shade with the same care. Everyone has melanin.

Will MELAN ever suggest lightening my skin?

Never. MELAN never suggests bleaching or lightening your skin tone. Where it helps fade dark marks, the goal is always your own natural tone — never lighter.

Is MELAN a medical app?

No. MELAN is a cosmetic skin wellness app and doesn't diagnose conditions. When something looks like it needs a professional, it tells you to see a dermatologist.

Who is SIMI?

SIMI is MELAN's skin guide. Ask it about a product, a routine step or a mark that won't fade, and it gives you a straight answer.

Is MELAN on iPhone?

Yes. Open app.melan.app in Safari: it's the same app as on Android, and the same account works on both. To keep it with your other apps, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
